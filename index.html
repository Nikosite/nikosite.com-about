WHAT INSPIRED YOU TO CREATE IT?
It would be an ancient pop culture website ran by an American - "sydlexia.com". But... it's not that simple. When I was seventeen years old, a thought occured to me. I loved making joke reviews, so much so that I wanted to go back creating a website. Yes, and before that concept was even a thing, I have been wishing to create a website for a while. First time, it was just teenage shit, and it's on a need-to-know basis. I ditched this hobby because I didn't realize that the sole requirement for the tasks of running a site, is to pay for the fucking domain. Well that was one hell of a fucking bummer I had to find out, the audacity for the need to use real money for an entirely free, fun hobby... Greed does kick in when you're rich. Fuck these greedy, miserable pieces of shit, I'm going to find a way no matter what. And I did, but for the second time, I lacked plans, decent coding skills, good design, and just... well, not a single good idea was popping up in my head. I can't find proof for you, since it's been deleted, and the only thing I can try proving it to you as if that can be clarified as evidence, is a drawing of a stick man with sunglasses, two Silenced MAC-10s and his... realistic penis drawn by my ex-partner. Not my finest hour. Now, what the fuck does my inspirations have to do with a picture of an actual uncircumcised penis on a stick man, drawn by a frequent reminder of my sheer stupidity? Believe it or not but I had a passion for programming, I still do though and it's why I created an account in Scratch years ago. My first project is actually a videogame idea of mine, next was another game project (barely a game) that was the drawing of a stick man it was originally featured, without the penis part, obviously. Then I created a project of what it is now my own website. Well... not really. First off, it was about joke reviews. Second, the design barely had a direction, the cover art of a game is on the left side of the review, on the right side is itself a review. And in the cons/pros, there is critical information based on my experiences. But... that's really all about it. I wish I could show all of this to you but it's all gone, I didn't even bother archiving it because I didn't think they were worthy of that. Oh well, a year later, thankfully, this pain in the gulliver is now long gone, thanks to my newly reformed ambition. So... yeah, I had this concept long before I was able to reach consciousness, thanks to all my loved ones for supporting me, I wouldn't have done it without them.
